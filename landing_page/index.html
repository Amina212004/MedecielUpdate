About Us

The ESI Medical Clinic provides healthcare services to students and staff of the Higher School of Computer Science of Sidi Bel Abbès.

Our mission is to support academic success by promoting physical and mental well-being through accessible, confidential, and professional medical care. We offer general consultations and preventive care tailored to the needs of the university community.
